Privacy

What this site collects about you: almost nothing.

The short version

This site does not ask for your name or email, has no accounts, sets no tracking cookies, shows no ads and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. It does not sell or share any information about you.

What your browser keeps

Nothing. The site saves nothing in your browser and sets no cookies. If you used notes or bookmarks in an earlier version of the site, those were kept only on your own device; you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

What the host sees

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (and a copy on claude.ai). Like any web host, they may record technical information such as your IP address and browser type to keep the service running and secure. This is covered by their own privacy statements: GitHub's Privacy Statement and Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

Fonts and outside requests

The site uses the fonts already on your device and loads nothing from other websites while you read. Links to original letters, talks and books take you to other sites, which have their own policies.

Search engines

If you arrive from a search engine, that search engine's own policy applies to your search.

Children

The site is general educational material and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if a visitor counter is added, this page will be updated first and will say exactly what is collected.
