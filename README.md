# Great Investors Library

Learn investing from Warren Buffett, Charlie Munger and Peter Lynch, in plain words. Free.

- **Warren Buffett:** his 30 letters to Berkshire Hathaway shareholders (1995 to 2024), as 14 big ideas and 127 lessons.
- **Charlie Munger:** his talks and letters (1982 to 2014), as 14 big ideas, 134 lessons and his 25 mental traps.
- **Peter Lynch:** his books and talks, as 12 big ideas, 86 lessons and seven hands-on tools.
- **The Timeline:** all three lives side by side from 1924 to today, every market storm since 1929 and what each of them did, and a "could you have held on?" test.

Open the site: https://greatinvestorslibrary.github.io

## What is in this folder

- `index.html` is the interactive library (one file, works offline).
- `buffett.html`, `munger.html`, `lynch.html`, `timeline.html` and the `ideas/` folder are plain reading pages, one per investor and one per big idea.
- `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` help search engines find every page.
