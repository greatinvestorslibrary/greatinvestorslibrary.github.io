Philip Fisher: Don't follow the crowd

Prices swing with fashion. Look years ahead, buy when fear runs high, and judge price by tomorrow's earnings, not today's.

  • War scares. Don't be afraid of buying when war fears push prices down. (Book, 1958)
  • Time as well as price. With a true growth company, waiting for a perfect price can cost more than paying a fair one. (Book, 1958)
  • Years ahead. He came to see that a company's price against its earnings a few years out matters far more than today's figure. (Book, 1980)
  • Against fashion. Good moments to buy often come when a passing problem makes the market think less of an outstanding company than it deserves. (Book, 1958)

Ask yourself

  • What does the crowd dislike right now? Is it right?
  • What would a company you like earn in three years?
