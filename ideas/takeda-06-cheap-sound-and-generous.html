Wahei Takeda · Big idea 6 of 10

Wahei Takeda: Cheap, sound and generous

He wanted shares that were cheap against earnings, companies with strong balance sheets, and managers who shared profits with shareholders.

  • About ten times. Ideally he paid about ten times earnings, which he linked to a roughly 10% earnings yield. (Interview, 2015)
  • Two numbers. He watched the payout ratio, how much profit goes to dividends, and the equity ratio, a sign of financial strength. (MUFG, 2024)
  • Skill shows. He used return on equity to find companies that were both profitable and cheap. (Interview, 2015)
  • Never overpay. He warned that buying overpriced shares always loses money in the end. (Interview, 2015)
“割高な株を買うといつか必ず損をします”Wahei Takeda, “Buy overpriced shares and you will lose, sooner or later.” Gendai Business interview, 2015 (our translation)

Ask yourself

  • What does a company you own pay out of each yen of profit?
  • What price would you pay for ten yen of yearly earnings?
