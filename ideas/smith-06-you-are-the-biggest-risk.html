Terry Smith: You are the biggest risk

Most damage to investors' returns is self-inflicted: chasing fashion, trading too much, buying what they don't understand.

  • Look in the mirror. His Owner's Manual warns that the greatest threat to your returns is your own behaviour. (Owner's Manual)
  • Not because it's fashionable. The fund will not own something because it is in fashion and might go up, because eventually it goes down, usually by a lot. (Owner's Manual)
  • Understand it or skip it. If you can't explain how an investment works, don't buy it. Complexity often hides risk. (FT article, 2013)
  • Tax is not a reason. Never invest just to avoid tax. Tax-driven schemes often carry high fees and weak returns. (FT article, 2013)
“The greatest threat you face to your investment performance is from you.”Terry Smith, Fundsmith Owner's Manual
“If you don't fully understand it, don't invest”Terry Smith, Ten golden rules, Financial Times, 2013

Ask yourself

  • What investment have you bought mainly because others were buying it?
  • Can you explain each of your investments in two sentences?
