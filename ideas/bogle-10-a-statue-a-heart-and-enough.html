John Bogle: A statue, a heart, and enough

He took far less from his work than he could have, survived years of heart disease, and was honoured by Buffett as the person who did most for American investors.

  • The statue. Buffett wrote that if a statue is ever built for the person who did most for American investors, it should be of Bogle. (Buffett, 2017)
  • A modest fortune. Buffett noted that Bogle's low-cost funds earned him only a tiny share of the wealth that flowed to managers who charged far more. (Buffett, 2017)
  • A new heart. He received a heart transplant in 1996, a month after handing Vanguard to John Brennan. (Vanguard, 2019)
  • No more to give. In 2012 he said his one regret was not having more to give away. (Telegraph, 2019)

Ask yourself

  • How much is enough for you?
  • What would you do with your work if money were not the goal?
