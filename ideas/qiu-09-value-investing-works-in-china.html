Qiu Guolu: Value investing works in China

He has tried most methods. For most people, he concluded, only value investing can really be learned and used, in China as anywhere.

  • First money in 1992. At 19 he queued overnight for share subscription certificates and made his first stock market gains. (Profile, 2015)
  • Many methods tried. Tips, charts, quant trading, concentrated picking, growth investing: he tried them all, and concluded that for most people only value investing can be learned and used. (Book, 2014)
  • Six strong years. The 2015 profile credits him with six strong years at China Southern, using value methods in China's A-share market. (Profile, 2015)
  • The traits behind it. The same profile sums up his habits: independent judgement, faith in common sense, a contrarian streak and patience for delayed rewards. (Profile, 2015)
  • Simple, not easy. The point of the sequel: the principles are simple; holding to them, and applying them flexibly in a noisy market full of traps, is not. (Book, 2018)
  • Always learning. He enjoys investing as a search for the truth, with new industries to learn every year. (Interviews, 2018–19)

Ask yourself

  • Which investing methods have you tried? Which could you actually keep using?
  • What would make a method work in one country but not another?
