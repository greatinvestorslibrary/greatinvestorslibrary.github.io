Walter Schloss: Paid only when partners gained

He charged no management fee. He took a share of profits only, and kept his costs tiny.

  • No fee. He charged no management fee, taking a 25% share of the profits instead. (Morningstar)
  • Not a dime. Buffett wrote that he took nothing unless his partners made money. (Buffett, 2007)
  • $11,000. Buffett once pointed out that his office costs were about $11,000 in a year when the partnership earned $19 million. (Morningstar)
  • Skin in the game. He invested his own money alongside his partners. (Morningstar · Buffett, 2007)

Ask yourself

  • How do the people managing your money get paid?
  • Are they paid when you lose?
