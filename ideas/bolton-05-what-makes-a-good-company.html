Anthony Bolton: What makes a good company

Start with the quality of the business, then ask whether it will be worth more in ten years.

  • Franchise first. Begin by judging how strong the business franchise is. (Book, 2009)
  • Ten years from now. Ask whether the company will still be here in ten years, and be more valuable. (Book, 2009)
  • Master of its own fate. Prefer companies in control of their own destiny, rather than at the mercy of others. (Book, 2009)
  • Managers who know the detail. Check that the people in charge understand the business strategically, operationally and financially. (Book, 2009)

Ask yourself

  • Which company you know will clearly be worth more in ten years? Why?
  • What could take a company's destiny out of its own hands?
