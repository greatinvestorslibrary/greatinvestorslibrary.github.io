André Kostolany: Sleeping pills and trams

His most famous advice: buy good shares, take sleeping pills, and stop looking. And never chase a share, any more than a tram.

  • Sleeping pills. Buy shares, take sleeping pills, don't look for years, and you will be rich. (extraETF · justETF)
  • Pain, then money. Stock market profits are payment for pain: first comes the pain, then the money. (justETF)
  • Don't chase. Never run after a tram or a share; another one always comes. (comdirect)
  • Patience pays. He liked to quote old Frankfurt brokers: money is made by sitting still, not with the head. (FBV, guide)
“First comes pain, then comes money.”André Kostolany, A saying of his, as translated by justETF
“Einer Straßenbahn und einer Aktie darf man nie nachlaufen.”André Kostolany, “Never run after a tram or a share.” As quoted by comdirect (our translation)

Ask yourself

  • How often do you check prices?
  • When did you last chase a share, and how did it end?
