Anthony Bolton: The contrarian's edge

The best bargains are usually in companies the crowd has given up on. The hard part is buying when it feels uncomfortable.

  • Popular means pricey. When everyone likes a share, the good news is already in the price. When everyone hates it, a little good news can lift it a lot. (Podcast, 2025 · Morningstar)
  • Discomfort is a signal. He bought recovery shares before all the information was in, a place he called the discomfort zone. (Morningstar)
  • But don't rush. He warned that buying too early is a common mistake. Be patient with your entry, and add more only when conviction is high. (Morningstar)
  • The tech bubble test. In the late 1990s he refused internet shares. His fund lagged the market by almost 20% before the bubble burst and proved him right. (Morningstar)
  • Unloved is not enough. Going against the crowd only pays when you know why the crowd is wrong. A share can be unpopular for good reasons. (Book, 2009)

Ask yourself

  • Which company do most people around you dislike right now? Do they have a good reason?
  • Could you hold a share for two years while everyone says you are wrong?
