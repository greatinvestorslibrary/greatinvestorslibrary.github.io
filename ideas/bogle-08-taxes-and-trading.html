John Bogle: Taxes and trading

Trading costs money twice: in commissions and in taxes. Low turnover keeps more of the return working for you.

  • Uncle Sam. One chapter's title tells investors not to pay the tax authorities any more than they should. (Book, 2007)
  • Turnover. Funds that trade a lot pass on costs and taxes that an index fund, which rarely sells, largely avoids. (Book, 2007)
  • A trader's tool. He was wary of exchange-traded funds when they were used for frequent trading rather than holding. (Book, 2007)

Ask yourself

  • How often do you trade? What did it cost last year, including tax?
  • What would happen if you traded half as often?
