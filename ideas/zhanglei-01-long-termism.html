Zhang Lei: Long-termism

Put your time and conviction into things that create value over many years. A long horizon is an edge from the starting line.

  • His compass. Long-termism means putting time and conviction into things that keep creating value, learning the most effective ways of thinking, and always seeking the truth. (Preface, 2020)
  • An edge from the start. A long horizon gives you an advantage before you begin, because so few investors can afford to wait. (Lecture, 2015)
  • Not about the shortest path. Yale taught him that investing is not a race for quick returns but working with the best people to build lasting organisations. (Yale Daily News)
  • Holding long is a result. Long holding periods are the result of backing companies that keep creating value, not a goal in themselves. (Book, 2020)
  • A friend of time. He sums up his approach in three phrases: long-termism, walking with people of great vision, and being a friend of time. (Book, 2020)
“investing is not about chasing the shortest path to returns”Zhang Lei, Email to Yale Daily News

Ask yourself

  • Which of your holdings will be more valuable in ten years, and why?
  • What short-term worry has pushed you off a long-term plan?
