Qiu Guolu: Pricing power is the core advantage

A company with a real edge can raise prices without losing customers. That power comes from doing what you can repeat and others cannot copy.

  • Two tests. A company with a real edge does something it can repeat again and again, and something others cannot copy. (Book, 2014)
  • Concentration is not enough. He cares less about how few rivals there are than about the bargaining power and irreplaceability that brings. (Lecture, 2017)
  • Easy money versus hard work. That power is the dividing line between businesses that earn easily and those that work hard for little. (Lecture, 2017)
  • Where it comes from. Scarce resources, a strong brand, patents, a dominant position or a licence: in the end each shows up as pricing power. (Book, 2014)
“定价权是核心竞争力”Qiu Guolu, “Pricing power is the core competitive advantage.” Gaoli lecture, 2017 (our translation)

Ask yourself

  • Which company you buy from could raise its prices tomorrow without losing you?
  • What does it do that nobody else can copy?
