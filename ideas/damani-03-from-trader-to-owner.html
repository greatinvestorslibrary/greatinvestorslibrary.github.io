Radhakishan Damani: From trader to owner

Influenced by Chandrakant Sampat, he moved from trading to owning quality businesses for years. Fund managers said they could never tell which hat he was wearing.

  • Sampat's influence. He has often said he was inspired by Chandrakant Sampat, whom he met in the early 1990s. (Profile)
  • HDFC Bank. By market folklore he was the largest individual shareholder of HDFC Bank at its 1995 listing, and kept buying. (BS, 2017)
  • Quality costs more. He was willing to pay up for HDFC Bank; in his view, quality commands a premium. (BS, 2017)
  • Both at once. A fund manager said it was impossible to follow him, never knowing whether he was investing or trading in a share. (BS, 2017)

Ask yourself

  • Do you know, for each holding, whether you are trading or investing?
  • Would you pay more for quality? How much more?
