John Bogle: A duty of trust

He believed managing other people's money is a duty, not just a business, and spent his later years saying so.

  • The 1987 speech. He told the fund industry it must change its pricing, products and promotion, and hold itself to higher standards of trust and fiduciary duty. (Vanguard, 2019 · NAPA, 2019)
  • A profession first. He argued fund management should behave like a profession rather than a mere industry. (NAPA, 2019)
  • Integrity. Morningstar wrote that admirers and critics alike agreed he was a man of irreproachable integrity. (Morningstar, 2019)
“We must hold ourselves to higher standards, standards of trust and fiduciary duty.”John Bogle, Speech to the fund industry, 1987, quoted by Vanguard

Ask yourself

  • Does the firm that manages your money act like a professional or a salesman?
  • What would a duty of trust mean in your own work?
