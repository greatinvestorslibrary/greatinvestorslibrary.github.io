André Kostolany: Money plus psychology

He said market trends come from two things: how much money is available, and the mood of the crowd.

  • The formula. Trend equals money plus psychology. (FBV, guide)
  • Not more informed. Don't assume people buying in a frenzy know more than you. (justETF)
  • Crises and rescues. He argued that in a crisis governments and central banks act, which is why to buy then. (justETF)

Ask yourself

  • Is money easy or tight right now?
  • Is the crowd fearful or greedy?
