Duan Yongping: NetEase, Apple and Moutai

Three bets made him famous as an investor. Each fits his rules: a business he understood, a culture he liked, a sensible price.

  • NetEase. He bought heavily when the shares were in panic and the company held more cash than its market value. They rose about twentyfold in six months and more than a hundredfold in all. (Interview, 2025 · AFP, 2017)
  • Sold too early? He later sold, saying he felt uneasy holding so much, and admits the later share price suggests he sold too soon. (Talk, 2018)
  • Apple. He began buying in 2011, seeing the shift to software and the platform, and a culture that puts users first and refuses products that add too little. (Interview, 2025)
  • Moutai. A unique product whose taste does not change, loyal customers, and steady dividends and buybacks. (Interview, 2025)

Ask yourself

  • What did all three companies have in common?
  • Which company near you holds more cash than the market thinks?
