Li Lu: Research like an owner

Study a company as if you had just inherited it and had to run it. Talk to everyone, from the boss to the security guard.

  • Imagine you inherited it. He suggests picturing that the company has just become yours and you must learn everything about it. (Lecture, 2019)
  • Talk to everyone. He talks to people at every level, down to security guards, and asks about hiring, pay and how staff are treated. (Lecture, 2019)
  • The two petrol stations. Two identical petrol stations drew very different custom, one about four times the other. The difference was an owner who ran his with care. So ask: do managers think like owners? (Lecture, 2019)
  • Talk to operators. His ideas come from wide reading, newspapers and annual reports, and from people who run businesses, more than from other investors. (Interview, 2013)
  • When you can't judge the boss. Management matters but is hard to assess. When he cannot judge it, he leans on the quality of the business and the price. (Interview, 2013)

Ask yourself

  • If you inherited a company you own shares in, what would you want to know first?
  • Who, besides the boss, could tell you the truth about a company?
