Peter Cundill · Big idea 1 of 10

Peter Cundill: A $500 lesson

His first investment, as a summer office boy, was a mining tip. He lost all $500 in under two days, and never forgot it.

  • Montreal. He was born in Montreal in 1938 and studied commerce at McGill, graduating in 1960. (McGill, 2011)
  • The tip. As an office boy in 1959 he put $500 into a speculative mining share and lost it all within 48 hours. (Globe, 2011)
  • An accountant. He qualified as a chartered accountant before moving into investment work. (McGill, 2011)

Ask yourself

  • What was your first investing loss?
  • What did it teach you?
