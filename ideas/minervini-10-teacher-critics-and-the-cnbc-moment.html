Mark Minervini: Teacher, critics and the CNBC moment

He now writes books and sells trading education. His record has fans and critics, and a 2021 TV interview showed the limits of trading charts without knowing the business.

  • The books. Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard (2013) and Think & Trade Like a Champion (2017) set out his method. (Goodreads · Stockopedia)
  • A business. He runs Minervini Private Access, a paid platform where members follow his trades, and live workshops. (Author page)
  • Upstart. In October 2021 a CNBC interview about Upstart, a stock he held, went badly, and his angry replies online drew more criticism. (investingLive, 2021)
  • Know the odds. Most individuals who try trading lose money, as SEBI's 2024 study showed in India. (SEBI, 2024)

Ask yourself

  • How should you weigh advice from someone who sells courses?
  • Should a trader understand the business behind a stock?
