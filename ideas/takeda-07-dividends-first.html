Wahei Takeda: Dividends first

Dividends, not price gains, were the core of his returns. He noted that dividends hold up far better than prices in bad times.

  • The main source. Dividends were his main return; in 2015 he expected about 350 million yen of tax on them. (Interview, 2015)
  • Steadier than prices. Even if a share price halved, he said, dividends usually fell by no more than about 10%. (Interview, 2015)
  • Steady growth. He aimed for companies with steady future growth that would pay good dividends. (Profile, 2015)
  • Glad for them. He chose companies whose rising profits he could be truly happy about. (Profile, 2015)

Ask yourself

  • How much of your return comes from dividends?
  • Which company's success would make you genuinely glad?
