Duan Yongping · Big idea 7 of 10

Duan Yongping: Hold great companies

Good companies rarely need selling. He sells when the business changes or something clearly better appears.

  • No need to sell. By 2018 he had never sold Apple, saying good companies don't need selling. (Talk, 2018)
  • Two reasons to sell. The company's earning power changes, or a clearly better company he understands appears at a good price. (Talk, 2018 · Interview, 2025)
  • Fully invested. He calls himself a full-position investor and dislikes idle cash, since bank interest earns less than good companies. (Interview, 2025)
  • Moutai at half price. When Moutai's shares fell by about half, he held on and kept buying. (Interview, 2025)
  • Trimming, not trading. By 2025 he thought Apple's shares expensive and was trimming by selling call options. (Interview, 2025)

Ask yourself

  • Which of your holdings would you happily own for ten years?
  • Have you ever sold a great company only because its price rose?
