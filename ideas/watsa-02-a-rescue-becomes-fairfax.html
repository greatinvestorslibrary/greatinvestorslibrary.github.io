Prem Watsa: A rescue becomes Fairfax

In 1985 he and his partners took over a small trucking insurer close to failure, renamed it Fairfax, and built it into a global insurance group.

  • His own firm first. In 1984 he started an investment firm, Hamblin Watsa, with his former boss. (Encyclopedia)
  • A near-bankrupt start. In 1985 they took control of Markel Financial, a struggling Canadian trucking insurer, and renamed it Fairfax. (Encyclopedia)
  • The insurance engine. Like Berkshire, Fairfax invests the money it holds for future claims, called float. (Letter, 2025)
  • Forty years on. By 2025 Fairfax earned a record $4.8 billion in a year. (Letter, 2025)

Ask yourself

  • What does an insurer do with premiums before claims are paid?
  • Why might a rescue be a good place to start?
