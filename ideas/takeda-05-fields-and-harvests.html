Wahei Takeda: Fields and harvests

He saw investing as farming. Shares are the field and dividends the harvest. A good farmer watches the harvest, not the price of the land.

  • The farm. Stocks are the field and dividends the harvest; watch the yearly harvest, not the price of the land. (MUFG, 2024)
  • Sow and replant. Plant seeds in companies likely to bloom, take your share of the fruit, then plant again. (MUFG, 2024)
  • Never withdrawn. He reinvested his dividends again and again, and his holdings grew past 10 billion yen. (MUFG, 2024)
  • The right season. His interviewer titled the book with a gardener's rule: don't sow flower seeds in midsummer. (Book, 2008)

Ask yourself

  • Do you check your dividends as often as your prices?
  • What would you plant now, and what would you wait to plant?
