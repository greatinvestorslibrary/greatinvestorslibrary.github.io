Terry Smith: The numbers that show quality

Every year he publishes the same few measures for his companies beside the market's. Quality shows up in the numbers.

  • Returns. In 2025 his companies averaged a 31% return on capital, against 17% for both the S&P 500 and the FTSE 100. (Annual letters)
  • Margins. Gross margin 62% against 45% and 43%; operating margin 28% against 18% and 17%. (Annual letters)
  • Cash. They turned 94% of their profits into cash. (Annual letters)
  • Safety. Median interest cover was 29 times, against 9 and 8: profits dwarf interest bills. (Annual letters)
  • Durability. The average company in the fund was founded in 1919. (Annual letters)

Ask yourself

  • Find the return on capital of a company you know. Is it above 17%?
  • Why might a company founded a century ago still be a good investment?
