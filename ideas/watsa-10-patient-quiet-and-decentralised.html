Prem Watsa · Big idea 10 of 10

Prem Watsa: Patient, quiet and decentralised

He avoids publicity, runs Fairfax through independent managers, and thinks in decades.

  • Decentralised. Fairfax's companies are run by their own leaders, with the head office judging them over the long term. (Letter, 2025)
  • Soundly financed. Always being soundly financed is one of the company's core principles. (Principles)
  • Honours. He was appointed a Member of the Order of Canada in 2015. (Encyclopedia)

Ask yourself

  • Do you give the people you back enough room?
  • What would you build if you thought in forty-year spans?
