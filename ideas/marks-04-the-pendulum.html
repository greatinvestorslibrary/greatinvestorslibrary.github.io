Howard Marks: The pendulum

Investor psychology swings like a pendulum between greed and fear, optimism and despair. It spends little time at the sensible midpoint.

  • A chapter of its own. His book has a chapter on awareness of the pendulum. (Book, 2011)
  • Rarely in the middle. Moods overshoot in both directions; the extremes are where mistakes, and opportunities, are made. (Book, 2011 · Book, 2018)
  • Many cycles. His second book traces the cycles in the economy, in profits, in attitudes to risk, in credit and in investor psychology. (Book, 2018)

Ask yourself

  • Where is the pendulum today: greed or fear?
  • How did you feel at the last extreme?
