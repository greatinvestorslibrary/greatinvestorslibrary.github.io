Duan Yongping: Never borrow

His companies take no interest-bearing loans, and he never borrows to invest. Debt makes you faster in both directions.

  • No loans. BBK, OPPO and vivo take no interest-bearing loans, so they cannot die from running out of cash. He cites Munger: most failures come from borrowing too much. (Talk, 2018)
  • One soaking. Borrowing speeds up gains and losses alike. Getting your feet wet is unavoidable; getting soaked can be permanent. (Talk, 2018)
  • Crises as chances. With ample cash, every crisis became a chance to act rather than a threat. (Talk, 2018)
  • Missing out is fine. Missing some opportunities is acceptable, as long as you catch the right ones. (Talk, 2018)
  • Rivals gone, BBK still here. Most of his old competitors have disappeared; he believes avoiding debt is a big part of why his companies survived. (Talk, 2018)
“我们不贷款，有充裕的现金流”Duan Yongping, “We take no loans, and we have plenty of cash flow.” Stanford talk, 2018 (our translation)

Ask yourself

  • What would happen to your portfolio if prices halved tomorrow and you had borrowed?
  • Which opportunity did you miss without regret?
