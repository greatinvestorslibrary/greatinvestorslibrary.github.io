Prem Watsa: Eight dollars and a degree

He grew up in Hyderabad, son of a teacher, studied engineering at IIT Madras, and arrived in Canada with almost nothing.

  • A teacher's son. He was born in Hyderabad in 1950; his father taught English and maths and later ran a boys' school. (Encyclopedia · Horatio Alger)
  • IIT Madras. He graduated in chemical engineering from IIT Madras in 1971. (Encyclopedia)
  • Almost nothing. He reached Canada with only a few dollars and paid for his MBA by selling furnaces in winter and air conditioners in summer. (Encyclopedia)
  • Hard roots. His father had lost both his own parents at the age of three. (Horatio Alger)

Ask yourself

  • What did you have when you started?
  • Which hard years taught you the most about money?
