Wahei Takeda: Calm in storms

He used only spare money, never borrowed to the limit, and refused to panic. A share price, he said, may go up or down; either is fine.

  • Up or down. He shrugged off falls with a short verse: a share price is fine going down and fine going up. (Interview, 2015)
  • Lehman. In the 2008 crash his holdings showed large paper losses, and he held on. (Interview, 2015)
  • Spare money only. He invested only surplus funds and warned never to use margin to the limit. (Interview, 2015)
  • One book. He said his only information source was the Shikiho company handbook, with no special inside sources. (Interview, 2015)

Ask yourself

  • Is any of your investing money needed in the next few years?
  • How do you behave when your portfolio is down a third?
