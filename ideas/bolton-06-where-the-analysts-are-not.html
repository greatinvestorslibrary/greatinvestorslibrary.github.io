Anthony Bolton: Where the analysts are not

Medium and smaller companies, and those analysts have given up on, are where mispricing lives.

  • Less crowded corners. He leaned towards medium and smaller companies, which fewer analysts followed. (Book, 2004)
  • No forecasts, no fans. He liked companies that analysts had given up on, with few or no profit forecasts. (Morningstar)
  • Takeovers as a bonus. Owning many cheap, mid-sized companies meant some would be bid for. His book has a whole chapter on why it is good to have your shares bought out. (Book, 2009)
  • Information is everywhere. His book has chapters on where to find information and on the value of good brokers: do the reading others skip. (Book, 2009)

Ask yourself

  • Name a company you know that almost nobody writes about.
  • Why might a small company be priced less efficiently than a giant one?
