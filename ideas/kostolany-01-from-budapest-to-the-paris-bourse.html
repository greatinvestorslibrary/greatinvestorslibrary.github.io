André Kostolany: From Budapest to the Paris bourse

A wealthy boy who wanted to be an art critic, he was sent by his father to learn broking in Paris in the 1920s.

  • Not his first wish. He studied philosophy and art history and hoped to become an art critic. (etf.capital · Kagels)
  • Paris. His father sent him to Paris in the mid-1920s to train under the broker Adrien Perquel. (comdirect · etf.capital)
  • No economics. He never studied classical economics and mocked economists' forecasts all his life. (comdirect)

Ask yourself

  • What would you have been, if not what you are?
  • Do economists' forecasts change what you buy?
