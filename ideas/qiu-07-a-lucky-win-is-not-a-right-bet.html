Qiu Guolu: A lucky win is not a right bet

Judge your method, not this year's result. A sound process shows itself over five to ten years.

  • Two different things. Winning a bet and betting right are not the same: a good process can give poor results for a while, and a bad one can get lucky. (Book, 2014)
  • Five to ten years. A sound method gives good results with high probability over five to ten years, not six months. (Book, 2014)
  • Six or seven in ten. He reckons a sound method beats the market in about six or seven years out of ten, a poor one in three or four. (Book, 2014)
  • Why it keeps working. Citing Joel Greenblatt: value investing works over time precisely because it doesn't work every year. (Book, 2014)
  • Don't keep switching. Investors who keep changing methods tend to fail; successful ones stick with one. (Book, 2014)

Ask yourself

  • Was your best result last year skill or luck? How would you tell?
  • How many bad years would make you abandon your method?
