Stanley Druckenmiller: From a PhD to a bank in Pittsburgh

He studied English and economics at Bowdoin, started a PhD in economics, then left it in 1977 for a trainee job at Pittsburgh National Bank. Within a year he was running its equity research.

  • Born 1953. He was born on 14 June 1953 in Pennsylvania and grew up near Philadelphia; sources differ on whether he was born in Pittsburgh or Philadelphia. (Wikipedia · Motley Fool)
  • Bowdoin. He graduated from Bowdoin College in 1975 in English and economics. (MoneyWeek)
  • The PhD he left. He dropped out of a doctorate in economics at the University of Michigan in 1977 to join the bank. (MoneyWeek · Wikipedia)
  • Fast promotion. He became head of the bank's equity research after one year. (MoneyWeek · Wikipedia)

Ask yourself

  • What would you give up a safe path for?
  • How fast could you learn a job well enough to run it?
