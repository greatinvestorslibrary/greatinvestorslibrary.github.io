Duan Yongping: The mistakes he admits

He is frank about his errors. Most came when he speculated or strayed outside what he understood.

  • Baidu. A speculative bet on Baidu ended in a short squeeze that cost him $100 to 200 million. He calls it speculation, not investing. (Talk, 2018)
  • General Electric. He bought GE in 2008, sold a few years later, and says he never really grasped its business model. (Interview, 2025)
  • Oil. About $100 million put into an oil index lost more than $10 million before he sold. (Interview, 2025)
  • Smartphones. BBK was slow to see the shift to smartphones; losses in 2012 and 2013 drained much of its cash. (Interview, 2025)
  • Speculation versus investing. He says his real investing mistakes were few and small; the big losses came from speculation. (Talk, 2018)

Ask yourself

  • Which of your losses came from speculating rather than investing?
  • What rule would have prevented your worst mistake?
