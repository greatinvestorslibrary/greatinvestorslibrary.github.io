Prem Watsa: Own your mistakes

His letters take the blame for what goes wrong and share the credit for what goes right, including public apologies.

  • Blame and credit. In his letters he accepts the blame for disappointments and shares the credit for successes. (Encyclopedia)
  • A public apology. In 2009 he told shareholders Fairfax should have been far more careful with three failed value investments. (Globe, 2009)
  • BlackBerry. Fairfax was BlackBerry's top shareholder through its decline; he later wrote of it as a mistake. (Reuters, 2013 · Letter, 2024)
“We should have been a lot more careful”Prem Watsa, To shareholders at the 2009 annual meeting, as reported by the Globe and Mail

Ask yourself

  • When did you last write down your own mistake?
  • Would you apologise to your partners in public?
