Prem Watsa: Early looks like wrong

Being right too soon is painful. While he waited for the crisis, Fairfax's shares fell by about half, and critics mocked him.

  • Three lean years. Between mid-2003 and mid-2006 Fairfax's share price fell about 50% as the swaps held back profits. (Reuters, 2013)
  • Early, again. In 2012 he wrote that Fairfax continued to be early, and bearish, citing Graham. (Nasdaq, 2017)
  • Survive to be right. Staying soundly financed is what lets an early investor survive until the market comes round. (Principles)
“We continue to be early - and bearish!”Prem Watsa, Letter to shareholders, 2012, as quoted by Nasdaq

Ask yourself

  • Could you hold a view for three years while everyone says you are wrong?
  • When does being early turn into being wrong?
