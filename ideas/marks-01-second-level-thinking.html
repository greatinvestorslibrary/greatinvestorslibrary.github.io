Howard Marks: Second-level thinking

To do better than others you must think differently, and better. First-level thinking asks whether something is good; second-level thinking asks what is already in the price.

  • The first chapter. His book opens with second-level thinking: a simple, obvious view cannot beat the market, because everyone already holds it. (Book, 2011)
  • Different actions. If you think like everyone else you will act like them and get the same results; you have to think differently from the norm. (Talk)
  • The example. A first-level thinker says a company is good, so buy. A second-level thinker asks whether everyone agrees, and whether the price already reflects it. (Book, 2011)
  • Not simple. He warns that second-level thinking is hard work and that easy formulas are a sign of first-level thinking. (Book, 2011)
“You have to do different things and think differently from the norm”Howard Marks, Talks at Goldman Sachs

Ask yourself

  • What does everyone believe about a company you own?
  • Is that belief already in the price?
