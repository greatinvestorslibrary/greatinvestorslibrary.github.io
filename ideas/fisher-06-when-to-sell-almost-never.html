Philip Fisher: When to sell: almost never

If you bought the right company, the time to sell is almost never. He gave three reasons to sell, and dismissed the rest.

  • Reason one. You made a mistake: the company is not what you thought. (Book, 1958)
  • Reason two. The company has changed and no longer passes the fifteen points as it did. (Book, 1958)
  • Reason three. A clearly better opportunity appears; he thought this should be rare. (Book, 1958)
  • Not a reason. A big rise in price, or fear of a market fall, is not a reason to sell an outstanding company. (Book, 1958)
“the time to sell it is—almost never”Philip Fisher, Common Stocks and Uncommon Profits, 1958

Ask yourself

  • Have you sold a great company only because it rose? What happened next?
  • Which of his three reasons applies to anything you own today?
