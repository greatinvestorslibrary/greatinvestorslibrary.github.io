Peter Cundill · Big idea 7 of 10

Peter Cundill: Indiana Jones

He searched the world for bargains, often starting with the country whose market had done worst that year.

  • The nickname. He was nicknamed the Indiana Jones of Canadian money managers. (Morningstar)
  • The worst market first. He would look at the country with the year's worst stock market and put some money there. (McGill)
  • Japan. After the 1997 crash he found hundreds of Japanese shares selling below net cash, with strong balance sheets. (Interview, 2004)
  • 37%. In 2004 over a third of his fund was in Japan. (Interview, 2004)

Ask yourself

  • Which market is most hated this year?
  • Would you go there?
