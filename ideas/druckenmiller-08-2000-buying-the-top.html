Stanley Druckenmiller: 2000: buying the top

His most famous mistake: after selling technology shares in January 2000, he could not bear watching them rise, and bought back in at the very top in March. Quantum lost heavily and he left Soros.

  • In his words. He says he bought the top of the tech market in March 2000 in an emotional fit, after selling out in January. (Safal Niveshak)
  • The top. The Nasdaq closed at a record 5,048.62 on 10 March 2000, then fell 78.4% to 1,114.11 by October 2002. (Fortune, 2015)
  • The damage. By late April Quantum was down 22% for the year. (CNN Money, 2000)
  • He leaves. He resigned from Soros's firm on 28 April 2000. (Bloomberg, 2000)
“I bought the top of the tech market in March of 2000”Stanley Druckenmiller, On his worst mistake, 2013 interview, as quoted by Safal Niveshak

Ask yourself

  • Have you ever bought something because you could not stand missing out?
  • What rule would have stopped you?
