Duan Yongping: Buying a stock is buying the company

One sentence from Buffett changed how he invested. A share is part of a company, and a company is worth the cash it will produce from now on.

  • The sentence that clicked. In America he first tried charts and could make no sense of them, until Buffett's line that buying a stock is buying a company made everything clear. (Magazine, 2025)
  • Value is future cash. A company is worth all the cash it will produce from now until it ends, discounted back to today. (Book, 2025)
  • Fewer than one in a hundred. The idea sounds simple, he says, but perhaps fewer than 1% of investors truly understand it. (Interview, 2025)
  • The company's money, not the market's. Investors earn what their companies earn. People forever talking about bull and bear markets are really hoping to win other traders' money. (Xueqiu replies)
  • No forecast needed. He decides by comparing opportunities, not by predicting the economy. (Interview, 2025)
“能真懂这句话的人，可能不到1%”Duan Yongping, On 'buying a stock is buying the company': “Perhaps fewer than 1% truly understand this.” Interview, 2025 (our translation)
“投资赚的是公司赚的钱”Duan Yongping, “Investing earns the money the company earns.” Reply on Xueqiu, 31 December 2023 (our translation)

Ask yourself

  • For one share you own, how much cash does the business produce in a year?
  • Are you trying to earn what a company earns, or what other traders lose?
