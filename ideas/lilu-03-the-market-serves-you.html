Li Lu: The market serves you

Prices are there to use when they suit you. The market cannot tell you what a company is worth, and it punishes anyone who forgets that.

  • Servant, not master. Use market prices to buy and sell when they suit you; never take them as a verdict on value. (Lecture, 2015 · Talk, 2026)
  • You cannot have both. Many investors want the benefits of value investing and good results every quarter. Chasing both pushes you into speculation. (Talk, 2026)
  • The market finds your weak spots. Act outside what you understand, or let fear and greed run you, and the market will expose it sooner or later. (Lecture, 2015)
  • Few play this game. He estimates that only about one market participant in twenty invests this way. That is why the opportunity lasts. (Lecture, 2015 · Lecture, 2019)
“the market is there to serve you, not to instruct you”Li Lu, Graham & Dodd luncheon, Columbia, 2026

Ask yourself

  • When did a falling price last change your opinion of a business? Should it have?
  • What results would you need every quarter to feel calm? Is that realistic?
