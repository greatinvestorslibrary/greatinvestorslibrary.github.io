John Maynard Keynes: Words he never said

Keynes is the most misquoted investor of all. Checking who really said what is a good habit for every investor.

  • Not his line. "The market can remain irrational longer than you can remain solvent" has no solid source in Keynes's writings. The earliest record found is the American adviser Gary Shilling, in 1986. (Quote Investigator)
  • Nor this one. "When the facts change, I change my mind" is also widely credited to him, but scholars of his papers have found no primary source. (Quote Investigator)
  • What he did write. His real words, in chapter 12 of The General Theory, the 1938 memo and the 1934 letter, are sharper than the invented ones. Read them. (General Theory, 1936 · Memo, 1938 · Letter, 1934)
  • Check before you share. If a quote is too perfect and has no date or source, it is probably not genuine. (Quote Investigator)

Ask yourself

  • Which famous investing quote have you shared without checking it?
  • Where would you look to check who really said something?
