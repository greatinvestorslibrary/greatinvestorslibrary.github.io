Terry Smith: Don't over-diversify

Twenty or so well-chosen companies give most of the safety of spreading out. More than that, and you know each one less well.

  • About twenty is enough. Around 20 well-chosen shares capture most of the benefit of diversification. (FT article, 2013)
  • A concentrated fund. At the end of 2025 Fundsmith Equity held 29 companies. (Annual letters)
  • The 80th company. His Owner's Manual asks how much a manager can really know about the 80th company in a portfolio. (Owner's Manual)

Ask yourself

  • How many companies could you follow properly in a year?
  • When does spreading money out stop reducing risk?
