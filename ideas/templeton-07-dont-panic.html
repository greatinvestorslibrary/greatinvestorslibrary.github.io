John Templeton: Don't panic

When markets crash, don't sell in fear. And don't be gloomy too often: over time, optimism has paid.

  • Hold your nerve. In a crash, don't sell just because everyone else is. Sell only if you find something clearly more attractive. (Rules, 1993)
  • This time is different? He warned against investors who think this time is different when it is really a repeat of the past. (Rules, 1993)
  • Not too fearful. His last rule: do not be fearful or negative too often. Over decades, shares have risen despite many falls. (Rules, 1993)

Ask yourself

  • What did you do in the last big market fall?
  • What would you do if your shares fell 30% tomorrow?
