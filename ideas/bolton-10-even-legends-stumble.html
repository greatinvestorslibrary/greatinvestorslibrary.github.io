Anthony Bolton: Even legends stumble

His China fund went badly at first. How he explained it teaches as much as his years of success.

  • The comeback. In 2010 he came out of retirement to launch a China fund, a market he had not invested in before. (interactive investor)
  • What went wrong. He blamed a focus on smaller companies, too much weight in consumer shares, and borrowing to invest, all of which hurt in a falling market. (Trustnet, 2012)
  • New market, new rules. Accounting scandals at some Chinese companies listed in the US also hurt. Familiar methods need new checks in an unfamiliar market. (Trustnet, 2012)
  • He said sorry. He apologised to investors for poor results. By the time he retired in 2014 the trust had just about turned a profit. (interactive investor)

Ask yourself

  • What would you check more carefully before investing in a country you don't know?
  • Why might borrowing to invest turn a bad year into a terrible one?
