Zhang Lei: People first

Hillhouse's growth came mainly from people: the managers it hires and the entrepreneurs it backs.

  • Owners aligned with customers. Before investing, find out whether owners, directors and managers are long-term, intelligent and aligned with their customers' needs. (CFA Institute, 2018)
  • Who he hires. Intelligence, independent thinking and honesty count for more than experience or background. (CFA Institute, 2018)
  • Three intellectual habits. Curiosity, independence and honesty, plus empathy: qualities he looks for in people. (Lecture, 2015)
  • Quality time with quality people. Swensen's reason for backing him became Hillhouse's motto: spend quality time with quality people. (Tribute, 2021)
  • Entrepreneurs who invest. He describes Hillhouse as entrepreneurs who happen to be investors. (Lecture, 2015)
“Spend quality time with quality people.”Zhang Lei, Hillhouse's motto, in his tribute to Swensen, 2021

Ask yourself

  • Whose judgement would you trust with your savings, and why?
  • How can you tell if a company's managers are aligned with customers?
