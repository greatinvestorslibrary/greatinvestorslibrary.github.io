Li Lu · Big idea 7 of 10

Li Lu: Patient, then decisive

Great opportunities are rare. Wait calmly while nothing happens, then act firmly when one appears.

  • One idea in decades. Munger read Barron's for decades and found just one big idea in it. That is how rare the best chances are. (Lecture, 2019)
  • Both at once. Patience and decisiveness have to live together: wait without fidgeting, then move in size. (Lecture, 2019)
  • Talent takes years to show. It can take around fifteen years to tell real skill from luck; short-term results are often luck. (Lecture, 2015)
  • Each shot stands alone. Like golf, a good hole does not guarantee the next. Judge each decision on its own merits. (Lecture, 2019)
  • BYD. He was drawn to the battery and car maker by its founder's engineering record, its rise in a fierce market, and a margin of safety. Berkshire bought 10% in 2008 for about $230m and sold out by 2025 after a more than twentyfold rise. (Interview, 2013 · News, 2025)

Ask yourself

  • How many truly great opportunities do you think you will see in the next ten years?
  • What would you need to see to act in size?
