Duan Yongping: The stop-doing list

Doing the right thing mostly means refusing to do wrong things. Most people know what is wrong; they do it anyway for a short-term gain.

  • Not doing wrong. Most people can tell right from wrong. They do wrong things anyway because the short-term pull is strong, like smokers who know the harm. (Talk, 2018)
  • Stop early. When you realise something is a mistake, stop at once. Stopping is cheapest at the start. (Talk, 2018)
  • BBK's list. No selling on credit, no late payments to suppliers, no late wages, no haggling, no making products for others, no interest-bearing loans, no attacking rivals. (Magazine, 2025 · Talk, 2018)
  • Saying no to Walmart. He once refused an order for a million VCD players without even discussing price: haggling would only waste everyone's time. (Magazine, 2025)
  • Learned the hard way. Many items on the list came from painful lessons; each rule saves them from a class of mistakes. (Interview, 2025 · Magazine, 2025)

Ask yourself

  • Write down three things you will never do as an investor. Why each one?
  • Which of your habits do you know is wrong but keep anyway?
