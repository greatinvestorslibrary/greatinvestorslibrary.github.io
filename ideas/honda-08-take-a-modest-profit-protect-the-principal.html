Seiroku Honda: Take a modest profit, protect the principal

He followed a simple selling rule: take a 20% gain when it comes, and if a holding doubles, sell half so the original money is safe.

  • Twenty per cent. On some positions, if he reached a 20% gain, he took it in full and put the money on deposit, without chasing more. (JPX, education)
  • Sell half at double. If a holding more than doubled, he sold half at once, getting his original money back. (JPX, education)
  • Then relax. With the principal safe, a later crash could not hurt him, and further gains were a bonus. (JPX, education)

Ask yourself

  • Do you have a rule for taking profits, or do you decide in the moment?
  • How would you feel holding a double with your original money already back?
