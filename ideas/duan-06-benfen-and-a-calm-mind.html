Duan Yongping: Benfen and a calm mind

Benfen means doing what you should, even when nobody asked. Add a calm, ordinary mind and, he says, you have the whole secret of BBK.

  • Their only secret. Asked whether BBK's success came from advertising or incentives, he says no: it came from benfen plus a calm mind. (Talk, 2018 · Review, 2026)
  • Do the right thing, and do it right. He came across Peter Drucker's line as a student; it became BBK's motto. (Interview, 2025 · Review, 2026)
  • What great bosses share. He cites a study of top chief executives: their personalities differed in every way except one, integrity. (Talk, 2018)
  • Every word a promise. Don't deceive customers or investors; what you say, you must do. (Talk, 2018)
  • Trust before money. He left Subor because its owner broke promises about shares: a problem of trust, not money. (Interview, 2025)

Ask yourself

  • What does doing what you should mean in your own investing?
  • When did staying calm last save you from a mistake?
