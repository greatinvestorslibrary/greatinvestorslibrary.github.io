Seth Klarman: Cheap isn't enough

A cheap share can stay cheap for years. He looks for a catalyst, an event that can close the gap between price and value.

  • Not a strategy. In 2026 he said simply buying cheap things is not a strategy on its own. (Interview, 2026)
  • A catalyst. Baupost wants an identifiable event that can drive a turnaround within a year or two. (Interview, 2026)
  • In the book too. One chapter on where to look starts with catalysts, alongside market inefficiencies and institutional constraints. (Book, 1991)
“cheap is not really a strategy”Seth Klarman, Masters in Business interview, 2026

Ask yourself

  • What event could make the market see value in a share you own?
  • How long would you wait for it?
