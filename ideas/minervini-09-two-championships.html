Mark Minervini: Two championships

To prove his method with real money, he entered the U.S. Investing Championship twice and won both times: +155% in 1997 and a record +334.8% in 2021.

  • 1997. He won with a 155% return, trading only shares. (PR Newswire, 2022)
  • 2021. He won the $1 million-plus stock division with +334.8%, then a contest record. (PR Newswire, 2022 · Business Wire, 2022)
  • The race. He led all year: +111.4% after three months, +223.4% after six, +277% after nine and +325.2% after eleven. (Business Wire, Apr 2021 · Business Wire, Jul 2021 · Business Wire, Oct 2021 · Business Wire, Dec 2021)
  • Record broken. In 2024 J Law of Hong Kong beat his record with +353.9%. (Webull, 2025)

Ask yourself

  • Is one great year proof of skill, or could it be luck?
  • Why does real-money verification matter?
