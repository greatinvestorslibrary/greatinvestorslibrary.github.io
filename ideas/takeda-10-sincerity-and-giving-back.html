Wahei Takeda: Sincerity, and giving back

He believed sincerity passed from person to person brings success and happiness, ran a circle to develop young people, and gave back much of what he gained.

  • A chain of sincerity. His philosophy: a chain of sincerity brings success and happiness. (Interview, 2015)
  • Marowa-ko. He led a group called Marowa-ko to develop people, and returned his experience and wealth to society. (MUFG, 2024)
  • A hundred heroes in gold. He had gold coins struck of a hundred figures from Japanese history, displayed in a hall he planned in Nagoya. (Talk, 2011 · His book, 2005)
  • Remembered. He died on 21 July 2016, aged 83; fans thanked him for the biscuits and investors called him a model. (Obituary, 2016)

Ask yourself

  • Who passed sincerity on to you?
  • What would you build to develop the next generation?
