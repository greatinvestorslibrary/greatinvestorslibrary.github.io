Chandrakant Sampat: Muscle, fat or cancer

Not all growth is good. He sorted it into muscle, fat and cancer, and warned that nothing grows exponentially for long.

  • Three kinds of growth. Good growth is muscle, bad growth is fat, and some growth ultimately kills, like cancer. (Tribute, 2015)
  • Value, not volume. Gillette was his example: the number of razors sold grows slowly, but the value of each sale grows a lot. (Tribute, 2015)
  • Nature's accountant. He compared nature to a miserly accountant that punishes even the smallest extravagance. Businesses, he implied, are no different. (Tribute, 2015)
  • No exponential forever. In 2000 he told the software enthusiasts that nothing grows exponentially for long. (Round table, 2000)
“nothing in this world grows exponentially for a long time.”Chandrakant Sampat, Investor round table, January 2000

Ask yourself

  • Which kind of growth does a company you own have?
  • What happens to its returns if growth slows?
