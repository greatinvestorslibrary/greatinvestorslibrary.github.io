André Kostolany: Rich twice, broke in between

He made a fortune betting on falling prices around 1929, lost it, and came close to bankruptcy more than once.

  • 1929. He grew rich betting that share prices would fall around the 1929 crash. (Kagels · justETF)
  • Then lost it. Big bets that went wrong wiped him out more than once. (justETF · Kagels)
  • 51 to 49. He admitted being right on 51% of his trades and wrong on 49%. (comdirect)

Ask yourself

  • Could you live with being wrong half the time?
  • What does that say about sizing each bet?
