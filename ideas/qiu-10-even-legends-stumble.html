Qiu Guolu: Even legends stumble

After two hard years he wrote to investors about his mistakes, and what he would watch more closely.

  • Three lessons. Too much in Hong Kong shares, cutting holdings too early in 2020, and too little attention to policy, especially the regulation of internet companies. (Letter, 2022)
  • Buying too soon. After China's internet shares halved in late 2021 he added more, and they fell further. (Letter, 2022)
  • Look up at the sky. His conclusion: keep your eyes on the road, but look up at the sky too, meaning watch policy as well as companies. (Letter, 2022)
  • Still a value investor. He kept his faith in low valuations and his refusal to chase crowded favourites. (Letter, 2022)
“不只是低头看路，也要抬头看天”Qiu Guolu, “Don't only look down at the road; look up at the sky too.” Letter to investors, 2022 (our translation)

Ask yourself

  • What could change an industry's pricing power overnight?
  • When did you last add to a falling share too early?
