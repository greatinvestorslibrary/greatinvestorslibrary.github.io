Chandrakant Sampat: How many things can you do without?

One of India's richest investors lived simply, jogged daily and asked how little he needed, not how much he could have.

  • His question. Parag Parikh said Sampat's philosophy was how many things you can do without. (ET, 2015)
  • The morning run. For years he jogged about seven kilometres a day, from Haji Ali to Marine Drive. (ET, 2015 · Profile)
  • Against excess. He objected to excessive consumption, debt, money-printing and short-termism. (Tribute, 2015)
  • Think for yourself. Take the best ideas from great thinkers in every field, connect them yourself, and take nothing and nobody for granted. (Tribute, 2015)

Ask yourself

  • What could you do without, starting this week?
  • When did you last question something everyone takes for granted?
