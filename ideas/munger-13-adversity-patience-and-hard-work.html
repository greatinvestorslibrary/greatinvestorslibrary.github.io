Charlie Munger: Adversity, patience and hard work

Setbacks are certain, and many are unfair. What decides a life is the response to them, and the steady work done in between.

  • Don't stay down. Staying down after a setback was on his list of routes to misery. (HS 1986)
  • Unfair blows will come. Life is very likely to deliver terrible, unfair blows. Some people recover and others do not. He cited Epictetus on using each setback well. (USC Law 2007)
  • Assiduity. He liked the word because it meant sitting down and staying there until the job is done. (USC Law 2007)
  • Losses bite harder. Losses and near misses provoke outsized reactions. The most addictive forms of gambling are built on near misses. (PHM 2005)
  • Small steps go unnoticed. Change that arrives in small steps escapes notice, because each step is a small contrast with the last. (PHM 2005)

Ask yourself

  • Recall your worst year. What did you learn that a good year could not have taught you?
  • Which loss are you still refusing to accept, and what is that refusal costing?
