John Bogle: Owned by its investors

After losing his job in 1974, he built Vanguard differently: the fund company is owned by its funds, and so by their investors, not by outside shareholders.

  • Fired, then free. He lost his job running Wellington after the falling stock market of the early 1970s and a quarrel with his partners. (AP, 2019 · Morningstar, 2019)
  • A new structure. In most fund companies an outside management firm runs the funds for its own profit. At Vanguard the funds own the manager. (Vanguard, 2019)
  • Still unique. Morningstar called Vanguard the first, and still the only, mutually owned fund family. (Morningstar, 2019)
  • The name. He named it after Nelson's flagship at the Battle of the Nile. (Telegraph, 2019)

Ask yourself

  • Whose interests come first at the companies that manage your money?
  • How would you know?
