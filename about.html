About the library

  • A free, independent study room for learning from the world's great investors, in their own words and in plain ones.
  • Every investor gets one page, built the same way: a five-minute summary, the big ideas, a special section, their own words, the originals, a test and a plan.
  • It covers the United States (Warren Buffett, Charlie Munger, Peter Lynch, Benjamin Graham, Philip Fisher, John Templeton, John Bogle, Howard Marks, Seth Klarman and Walter Schloss, plus a Timeline of the first three lives), the United Kingdom (Anthony Bolton, Terry Smith and John Maynard Keynes, plus a UK Timeline), China (Li Lu, Duan Yongping, Qiu Guolu and Zhang Lei), India (Chandrakant Sampat, Parag Parikh and Radhakishan Damani) and Japan (Seiroku Honda, Wahei Takeda and Atsuto Sawakami). More countries will follow, chosen only where there is a public record of their own letters, books, talks or results, and a reputation for integrity.
  • Every summary is written in our own words. Exact words appear only as short lines, with their source. Each page links you to the originals.
